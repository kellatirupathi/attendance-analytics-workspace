      How your SPI is calculated

      1. Course Score for each course

      Classroom Quizzes × 10% + Module Quizzes × 15% + Skill Assessments × 25% + Final Skill Assessment × 50%

      For now: (Classroom × 10 + Module × 15) ÷ 25

      2. Course Score → Level and Points (table on the right)

      3. SPI = Total Points ÷ Number of courses
