How SPI is calculated

  1. 1Skill Track Score = Classroom Quizzes × 10% + Module Quizzes × 15% + Skill Assessments × 25% + Final Skill Assessment × 50%
  2. 2Skill Level and Points: the Skill Track Score maps to a level on the scale (right)
  3. 3SPI = Σ (Level Points × Course Weight) ÷ Total Weight

Provisional SPI: assessments not yet conducted are left out until they are held. Course weights are equal until confirmed. The SPI is final after the Final Skill Assessment. A score below 50% in an assessment type earns no SPI credit for it.
